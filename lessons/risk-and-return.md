# Lesson 3: Risk, uncertainty, and return

## Core idea

A potential return is uncertain. A responsible analysis considers not only what could go well, but also what could go wrong and how uncertain the assumptions are.

## Concepts

- **Risk:** The possibility that an outcome differs from what was expected, including loss.
- **Risk tolerance:** A person's ability and willingness to withstand uncertainty or loss.
- **Concentration risk:** Exposure that depends heavily on one asset, event, or outcome.
- **Leverage:** Using borrowed funds or financial exposure greater than the amount committed. It can magnify losses as well as gains.
- **Counterparty risk:** The possibility that another party in a transaction or service fails to meet its obligations.

## Safety checklist for learning

- Use hypothetical examples and simulations.
- Be cautious of guaranteed-return claims, urgency, and unsolicited investment offers.
- Never share passwords, one-time codes, private keys, or recovery phrases.
- Do not use borrowed money or money needed for essentials in speculative activities.
- Remember that a backtest or historical example does not guarantee future results.

## Reflection

Explain why leverage can magnify both gains and losses. Give a fictional example without using real funds.
