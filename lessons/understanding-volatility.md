# Lesson 2: Understanding volatility

## What is volatility?

Volatility describes the degree of variation in an asset's price over a period. A market with large and frequent price changes is often described as highly volatile.

Volatility does not tell us which direction a price will move next. A large movement can be upward or downward.

## Related concepts

- **Price:** The quoted value at a particular moment and venue.
- **Volume:** The amount traded during a defined period.
- **Liquidity:** The ability to transact without causing a large price impact.
- **Spread:** The difference between the best available buy and sell quotes.
- **Timeframe:** The interval represented by a chart candle or data point.
