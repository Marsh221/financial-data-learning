# Lesson 1: Financial literacy foundations

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to explain:

- The difference between an asset, a liability, income, and an expense.
- Why higher potential returns generally come with higher uncertainty.
- Why money needed for everyday essentials should not be exposed to speculative risk.
- The difference between investing, trading, and speculation.

## Key terms

- **Asset:** Something with economic value that a person owns or controls.
- **Volatility:** How much and how quickly a price changes over time.
- **Liquidity:** How easily an asset can be exchanged without greatly affecting its price.
- **Diversification:** Spreading exposure across different assets or categories to avoid relying on one outcome.
- **Drawdown:** A decline from a previous peak in the value of a portfolio or account.
- **Simulation:** A practice exercise using hypothetical money or data rather than real funds.

## A useful distinction

Investing usually refers to holding assets with a longer-term view. Trading generally involves more frequent decisions based on changing prices. Both involve uncertainty; neither guarantees a profit.
