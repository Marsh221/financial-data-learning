# Binance & Crypto Learning Journey — 90-Day Roadmap

**Status:** Planning document  
**Starting point:** Beginner  
**Primary objective:** Build crypto knowledge, protect starting capital, and develop a disciplined long-term investing process.  
**Currency context:** Kenya shillings (KES)  
**Review cadence:** Weekly

> **Important:** This is an education and planning roadmap, not a promise of returns or individualized financial advice. Crypto assets and platform products can lose value. No yield is guaranteed, and even stablecoins can depeg or face issuer, liquidity, custody, smart-contract, and platform risks. Only use money you can afford to lose.

## Guiding principles

1. Learn before committing meaningful money.
2. Protect essentials first: rent, food, bills, debt payments, and emergency savings come before crypto.
3. Never borrow to invest or use leverage to try to accelerate returns.
4. Avoid futures, margin, copy-trading signals, and unfamiliar high-yield schemes during the beginner phase.
5. Verify fees, withdrawal networks, product terms, lock-up periods, and redemption conditions before every transaction.
6. Use strong account security: unique password, authenticator or passkey where available, anti-phishing code, withdrawal controls, and careful address/network checks. Never share passwords, OTPs, seed phrases, or API keys.
7. Keep a record of every deposit, conversion, purchase, sale, fee, reward, and withdrawal.
8. Review decisions against a written plan rather than social-media hype or short-term price movements.

## The 90-day learning plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Days 1–14)

**Learn**
- What blockchain is and how Bitcoin and Ethereum differ.
- What a cryptocurrency, token, stablecoin, wallet, and private key are.
- What USDT is, how it aims to track the US dollar, and why it is not the same as cash or a bank deposit.
- The difference between Spot, Funding, and Earn balances on Binance.
- Market orders, limit orders, spreads, trading fees, withdrawal fees, and network selection.
- Account security, phishing, custody, and recovery procedures.

**Practice**
- Complete introductory lessons on Binance Academy.
- Explore the Binance interface without placing trades.
- Learn how to check product terms and supported withdrawal networks.
- Make a personal security checklist and enable available protections.

**Checkpoint:** Explain in your own words how a crypto transfer works, what USDT risk means, and how to avoid sending funds on the wrong network.

### Phase 2 — Research and first strategy (Days 15–30)

**Learn**
- Market capitalization, circulating supply, token unlocks, liquidity, trading volume, and volatility.
- How to research a project: purpose, team, documentation, tokenomics, adoption, security history, and risks.
- Long-term investing versus short-term speculation.
- Dollar-cost averaging (DCA), diversification, and the limits of past performance.
- How Binance Simple Earn and Auto-Invest work, including flexible/locked terms, eligibility, rates, caps, early redemption, and changing product availability.

**Practice**
- Compare Bitcoin and Ethereum using primary project documentation and reputable market data.
- Build a watchlist; do not buy simply because an asset is trending.
- Simulate a recurring investment plan in a spreadsheet before using real funds.
- Compare the displayed Earn rate with lock-up, redemption, asset-price, and platform risks.

**Checkpoint:** Write a one-page investment thesis and a list of reasons you would not buy an asset.

### Phase 3 — Portfolio and risk management (Days 31–60)

**Learn**
- Position sizing and how losses affect a portfolio.
- Concentration risk, correlation, drawdowns, and volatility.
- How to set a maximum amount of money exposed to crypto.
- How to calculate average entry cost, fees, and realized/unrealized profit or loss.
- Staking, Launchpool, and other Binance features: eligibility, reward mechanics, lockups, token risks, and changing terms.

**Practice**
- Maintain a paper portfolio or tracker before increasing real exposure.
- Record the reason, time horizon, risks, and exit conditions for every hypothetical purchase.
- Review holdings weekly, not compulsively throughout the day.
- If using a small real-money amount, keep it within a pre-set budget and do not increase it to recover losses.

**Checkpoint:** Produce a portfolio report showing contributions, current value, fees, rewards, and percentage gain/loss.

### Phase 4 — Review and long-term process (Days 61–90)

**Learn**
- Basic chart concepts: trend, support/resistance, volume, and common indicators, while understanding that charts cannot predict prices reliably.
- Market cycles and why timing the market is difficult.
- Profit-taking, rebalancing, tax records, and withdrawal planning.
- How to spot scams, fake support accounts, pump-and-dump schemes, guaranteed-return claims, and malicious wallet approvals.

**Practice**
- Write a personal investment policy: goals, time horizon, contribution limit, asset-selection rules, risk limits, and review schedule.
- Review whether any product or asset still fits the original thesis.
- Compare the results with simply holding cash or making no investment.
- Create a plan for securely withdrawing funds and maintaining records.

**Checkpoint:** Complete a 90-day review and decide whether to continue, adjust, or pause. Do not measure success only by short-term profit; measure learning, discipline, security, and risk control too.

## Earning and investing approaches to study

| Approach | What it does | Main risks / cautions |
|---|---|---|
| Long-term spot investing | Buy an asset and hold it | Price can fall sharply; no guaranteed recovery |
| DCA / Auto-Invest | Invest a fixed amount on a schedule | Does not prevent losses or guarantee a good average price |
| Simple Earn | Earn variable or stated rewards on eligible assets | Platform, issuer, liquidity, depeg, redemption, and product risks |
| Staking | Help secure a proof-of-stake network and receive rewards | Slashing, lock-up, validator, network, and price risks |
| Launchpool | Eligible users commit supported assets to receive project-token rewards | New-token volatility, eligibility, caps, and changing terms |
| Spot trading | Buy and sell assets without borrowing | Losses, fees, volatility, and emotional decision-making |

Treat any displayed APR/APY as variable unless the current product terms explicitly say otherwise. A higher rate usually comes with risks that must be understood; do not select a product on rate alone.

## Capital planning — not an instruction to invest

The first task is to establish a safe, affordable monthly amount after essentials and emergency savings. Starting small is sensible. There is no minimum amount that makes crypto profitable, and fees can make very small transactions inefficient.

An illustrative allocation discussed as a learning exercise was:
- 50% long-term investments
- 25% reserve
- 15% earning products
- 10% learning/experimentation

**This split is not a recommendation or a default portfolio.** Do not apply it until the user's starting capital, monthly contribution, risk tolerance, debts, emergency savings, time horizon, and local costs are understood. A cash reserve outside crypto may be more appropriate than holding a stablecoin on an exchange.

## Weekly routine

- **Session 1 — Learn (30–60 min):** Study one topic and write a short summary.
- **Session 2 — Research (30–60 min):** Review one asset or product using primary documentation and current terms.
- **Session 3 — Practice (20–30 min):** Update a paper portfolio or transaction tracker.
- **Weekly review (15 min):** Record what was learned, what changed, fees, risks, and next week's focus.

Do not make a trade just to complete a weekly exercise.

## Kenya-specific checks

Before depositing, trading, earning rewards, or withdrawing:
- Check current Kenyan laws, regulatory notices, and the status of relevant virtual-asset service providers using official Kenyan government and regulator sources.
- Check current Kenya Revenue Authority guidance and keep complete transaction records; obtain qualified tax advice for personal circumstances.
- Confirm Binance availability, account eligibility, payment methods, fees, and product access in the user's account. Features and terms can vary by location and change over time.
- Do not assume a product is locally authorized, protected like a bank deposit, or tax-free merely because it appears in the app.
